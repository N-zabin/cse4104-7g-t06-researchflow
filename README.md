# 🔬 ResearchFlow

### An Intelligent Research Discovery, Analysis and Collaboration Platform

ResearchFlow is an AI-powered research management platform designed to help students, researchers, and academic teams simplify their research activities. It aims to make academic paper discovery, document analysis, literature review, research gap identification, and collaboration easier through a unified platform.

---

## 1. Project Description

ResearchFlow is an AI-powered research management platform that helps students and researchers discover academic papers, analyze research documents, compare methodologies, identify potential research gaps, and collaborate in one workspace.
