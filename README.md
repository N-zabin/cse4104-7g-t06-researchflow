# 🔬 ResearchFlow – An Intelligent Research Discovery, Analysis and Collaboration Platform

## 1. Project Overview

ResearchFlow is an AI-powered research management platform designed to help students, researchers, and academic teams simplify their research activities.

The platform provides a unified workspace for academic paper discovery, PDF document analysis, literature review, methodology comparison, research gap identification, and academic collaboration.

By integrating Artificial Intelligence, ResearchFlow aims to reduce manual research efforts and make academic research more organized, efficient, and accessible.

## 4. Project Objectives

- To develop an intelligent platform for academic research management.
- To simplify academic research paper discovery and organization.
- To automate research paper analysis using Artificial Intelligence.
- To generate structured literature review matrices.
- To compare research methodologies and findings.
- To identify potential research gaps from existing research papers.
- To support collaboration among students and researchers.

## 5. Proposed Features

1. **User Authentication** – Secure user registration and login.
2. **Academic Research Paper Discovery** – Search and discover academic papers using external academic APIs.
3. **Multiple PDF Upload and Parsing** – Upload multiple research papers in PDF format and extract their text.
4. **AI-Powered Paper Summarization** – Generate summaries and extract important information from research papers.
5. **Literature Review Matrix Generation** – Organize research papers into a structured literature review matrix.
6. **Methodology and Comparative Analysis** – Compare research methodologies, findings, and limitations across papers.
7. **AI-Based Research Gap Discovery** – Identify potential research gaps based on analyzed papers.
8. **Research Trend Visualization** – Present research trends based on publication data.
9. **Collaborative Workspaces** – Provide shared workspaces for research team members.
10. **PDF Report Export** – Export research analysis and literature review results as PDF reports.
